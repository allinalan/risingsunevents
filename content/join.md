# Join the Rising Sun Events Team

## Joining RSD Show Team

This is a step-by-step process of starting with the Rising Sun Team. Make sure to complete all the following tasks below and you’ll start off the right way.

**— Have all these completed before 1st Team Meeting —**

### Step #1: Be at 15,000 in career sales AND attend RSD Events Training in August or January

### Step #2: Order Sharpeners & Admin on VectorConnect

- Item #82 Sharpener (via Vectorconnect: Cutco Orders - Non-Customer Orders - Buy Cutco Samples)
- Item #FS102 Ed Reed Sharpener (via Vectorconnect: Cutco Orders - Non-Customer Orders - Cutco Events Literature Order)
- Item #L17 - Cutco Events Order Form (pack of 50)
- Item #L419 - In-Home Sharpening Form (pack of 50)
- Item #L281 - Cutco Events Notepad (50 duplicate sheets)
- Item #L74 - Guarantee/DD Edge Sheet (laminated)
- Item #L355 - Family Program Brochure (laminate when it arrives)
- Item #L356 - Upgrade Program Brochure (laminate when it arrives)

### Step #3: Message Field Trainer(s) & Look Up Info for Event

- Click the “Team Contact Info” link on the homepage, find reps you’re field training with, and text them that you’re coming to field train.
- Google the info for the events you’re field training at for open/close times and locations (don’t ask the field trainer or Alan, must learn to find info personally).

### Step #4: Setup Schedule

- Click the “Important Dates” tab and put every team meeting for the campaign in your personal schedule.
- In-Person if you live within 1 hour of Office Address
- Via Zoom if you live outside of 1 hour of Office Address (you must be in meeting mode; no distractions and focus as if you’re in person)

SUNNY, the Team's Assistant

SUNNY is a web app for every rep on the team. No download and no account: the first time you open it, just pick your own name.

Open SUNNY

Add SUNNY to Your Home Screen

Add it once and it opens like an app.

iPhone

  • Open SUNNY in Safari
  • Tap the Share button
  • Tap Add to Home Screen

Android

  • Open SUNNY in Chrome
  • Tap the ⋮ menu
  • Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app)

What You Can Do With SUNNY

Ask SUNNY

Ask questions in plain words and get answers from the Team Policy and SUNNY's knowledge:

  • Event policies
  • Show charges and overages
  • Scripts
  • Mesa Marketplace
  • Shift picking and levels
  • Late fines
  • Shift coverage rules
  • Rep of Record rules
  • "When is the next meeting?" (from Important Dates)

ROR

Look up a customer's Rep of Record by name or address, with Call and Text buttons for that rep.

Shifts

  • See your own upcoming shifts from the Show Shift Board: who you work with and the show's status
  • See a show's crew by day
  • Freshmen and JV can request coverage for a shift (both reps confirm by text before anything moves)

Contacts

Find a teammate's phone number by name or nickname.

What SUNNY Doesn't Do

SUNNY doesn't assign shifts (go through Alan or Matt), change the schedule, or text anyone for you.
